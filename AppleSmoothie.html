Apple Smoothie

A refreshing, healthy & naturally delicious drink
to keep you fresh, active and full of energy.

Prep: 05 min Cook: 0 min Serves: 2

About This Drink

This Apple Smoothie is a perfect blend of fresh apples, creamy milk and natural goodness. It's a healthy, sugar-free drink made with real ingredients to keep you fresh, active and full of energy.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Apple
  • Liquid Milk
  • Ice Cream
  • Ice Cubes
  • Powder Milk (for better taste)
  • Condensed Milk (if you want)
  • Sugar Free
  • No added sugar

Instructions

  1. Wash and cut the apple into small pieces. Remove the seeds.
  2. Add apple pieces, liquid milk, ice cream, powder milk and condensed milk into a blender.
  3. Add ice cubes for extra chill and a thick, creamy texture.
  4. Blend everything on high speed until smooth and frothy.
  5. Pour into a glass and serve immediately. Enjoy your healthy, sugar-free Apple Smoothie!
Tip: For the best taste, use chilled milk and fresh apples. Serve immediately after blending.

Nutrition Per Serving

  • Calories: ~150 kcal
  • Protein: 4 g
  • Carbs: 22 g
  • Fat: 4 g
